Security monitoring rarely extends to the physical layer. *Alter* techniques hide data in a side channel of a legitimate signal through packet length extension, spectral sub-carriers, or modulation over modulation. *Mimic* techniques synthesize a non-protocol-compliant signal with a target's PHY signature, found through Bayesian optimization and neural parameter estimation from spectrograms. Measured by Fréchet Inception Distance, both are far closer to normal traffic than conventional low-probability-of-intercept signals, and a battery-powered SDR prototype reached a receiver approximately 300 m away.
